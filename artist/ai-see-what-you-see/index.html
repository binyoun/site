Artist / I am telling what a I* see is what you see

I am telling what a I* see is what you see.
You are reading what AI** hear is what you hear.

2022 · Video, AI-generated captions, narration

This video work invites viewers to float in the alienated ocean of an island, feeling how uncanny moments happen with AI-generated captions, over-layered moving images, and mysterious Korean narration.

The work is about the process of making captions in English based on auditory elements of Korean narration, a slippage between what I* (the human self) sees and what AI** hears.
