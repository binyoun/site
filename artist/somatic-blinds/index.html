Somatic Blinds

2022 · Installation, grow light, vinyl blind, plywood, eye hook, turnbuckle, aircraft wire, box fan, fishing wire, fishing hook, Mylar thermal blanket, bird reflective scare tape

As soon as the vessel became full of floating gas, I turned back. Then the light rimmed me from the back. Now, I see lines folded and unfolded, wrinkles layered and inscribed I haven't seen. I was blind.
